Request Account Deletion

Pregnancy Guide App — Community Account

💬 Which account does this apply to?

This page explains how to delete the Community account you created inside the Pregnancy Guide Book & Tips app (package name: com.minarlabs.pregnancy) to post, comment, and message other users.

If you never created a Community account and only use the core pregnancy-tracking features, there is no server account to delete — all that information already stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or use App Settings → Reset App.

📱 Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. 1Open the Pregnancy Guide Book & Tips app and go to the Community tab.
  2. 2Tap the settings (⚙) icon at the top of the Community screen.
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. 4Confirm when asked. Your account and Community data are deleted right away.

✉️ Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by emailing us. Please send your request from the same email address used to sign up, so we can verify it's you.

Email: minarhossin11@gmail.com
Subject: Delete My Community Account
Include: the name shown on your Community profile, and your account email.

We will process email requests within 7 business days and confirm by email once your data has been deleted.

📋 What gets deleted

✕ Your profile (display name, email, account)

✕ Your posts, comments, and likes

✕ Your ability to send or receive new messages

Kept for a short period:

✓ Messages you already sent may still appear in the other person's conversation thread, since deleting your account does not remove messages from someone else's inbox — this is standard behavior for any messaging feature.

No retention period beyond this applies — once your account is deleted, your profile and content are permanently removed from our database and cannot be recovered.

🔒 Other data (not affected by this request)

Your pregnancy tracker, diary, medicines, weight log, and doctor notes are stored only on your device and are not part of your Community account. Deleting your Community account does not affect this data. To remove it, use App Settings → Reset App on your device, or simply uninstall the app.

📧 Questions?

For anything else about your data or this process, contact us at minarhossin11@gmail.com. See our full Privacy Policy for more details.
